Featured Projects & Case Studies

Automated Manufacturing & Warehouse Fulfillment Terminal

Overview: Developed a standalone Python desktop application deployed on warehouse terminal computers that automates custom order routing, laser-etching manufacturing, and inventory tracking. The system integrates e-commerce data pipelines with physical warehouse hardware and creative automation scripting to enable a hands-free, error-proof manufacturing and picking workflow.

Challenge: A high-volume e-commerce merchant faced critical bottlenecks and an 18% picking and manufacturing error rate on personalised products. Custom orders required staff to manually open Adobe Illustrator, copy-paste custom text or design options, manually configure laser-etcher graphics, and manually track items across the warehouse floor, resulting in severe bottlenecks and delayed order dispatch times.

Approach & Solutions:

  • Data Injection (Shopify Liquid & Matrixify): Programmed custom Liquid properties to lock personalisation details onto order payloads. Orchestrated automated, scheduled data exports via Matrixify every 15 minutes to ensure data freshness.
  • Data Redundancy & Holiday Logic: Configured the pipeline to ingest 7 days of historical order data during every sync. This structural fail-safe ensures zero custom orders are dropped or missed during extended warehouse shutdowns or heavy backlogs.
  • Automated Laser Etching (JSX & Adobe Illustrator): Built an automated image processor using JSX (ExtendScript). When a warehouse worker scans a raw component's barcode, the desktop app communicates with Adobe Illustrator via JSX to dynamically generate customised artwork based on exact customer specifications, automatically formatting and prepping graphics for the laser etcher without human intervention.
  • Print-on-Demand Tracking: Upon successful etching, the terminal instantly prints a unique, customised barcode label corresponding specifically to that individual custom order, making downstream wave-picking, sorting, and packing entirely error-proof.

Results: Completely eliminated manual graphic prepping, drastically reduced manufacturing error rates, eliminated fulfillment bottlenecks, and enabled hands-free automated laser production.
